Night-Lite GR-Series Light Tower

Lead mechanical design involvement in the development of a mobile light tower, including structural, enclosure, mounting, and production-focused mechanical systems.

Project Overview

Served as Lead Design Engineer for the Night-Lite GR-Series light tower at Allmand / Briggs & Stratton.

Design responsibility included mechanical development across the frame, tower, enclosure, control box, engine and generator mounting, and shrouds.

The project required integrating multiple mechanical systems into a manufacturable product while considering component packaging, structural interfaces, assembly, and production requirements.
