Project 06 | Material Handling & Surface Protection

5 ft x 10 ft Sheet Flipper Modification

Modification of an existing sheet-handling fixture to accommodate larger stainless-steel sheets while improving control during material handling and laser-loading operations.

Project Overview

Modified an existing sheet-flipping fixture that was originally designed for 5 ft x 8 ft sheets so it could accommodate 5 ft x 10 ft stainless-steel sheet stock.

The fixture provides a controlled method for flipping large sheets before laser loading, allowing the shop to control which surface of the stainless sheet is exposed during subsequent handling.

The modification extended the capability of an existing piece of material-handling equipment rather than requiring a completely new fixture.

Impact

Expanded sheet-handling capability while supporting surface-quality control before laser cutting.

Problem Addressed

  • Existing fixture was configured for 5 ft x 8 ft sheet stock
  • Production needed to handle larger 5 ft x 10 ft stainless sheets
  • Large sheets required controlled handling during flipping
  • Surface condition needed to be considered during material handling

Design Response

  • Modified the existing fixture for 5 ft x 10 ft sheet compatibility
  • Retained the basic concept of the existing sheet-flipping equipment
  • Provided support appropriate for the larger sheet format
  • Maintained a controlled method for flipping and positioning sheets
