Project 03 | Assembly Tooling & Manufacturing Support

Damper Shaft Assembly Press Tooling

Dedicated tooling developed to improve control, alignment, repeatability, and organization during recurring damper-shaft assembly operations.

Project Overview

Developed dedicated tooling for installing seal assemblies, bronze bearing carriers, and bearings onto damper drive and idle shafts using a hydraulic press.

The tooling provides stable support beneath the shaft and component-specific press interfaces so axial installation forces can be applied in a more controlled and repeatable manner.

Dedicated storage locations were incorporated at the point of use so the required tooling remains visible, organized, and readily available for recurring assembly operations.

Impact

Improved assembly alignment, repeatability, tooling organization, and control during recurring shaft-assembly operations.

Problem Addressed

  • Components could become misaligned during installation
  • Shafts required stable vertical support beneath the press
  • Sensitive seal and bearing surfaces required controlled loading interfaces
  • Multiple assembly stages required different tooling configurations

Design Response

  • Dedicated lower shaft-support tooling
  • Component-specific upper press interfaces
  • Tooling developed for multiple stages of shaft assembly
  • Point-of-use storage for organization and visibility

Tooling Approach

The tooling system separates the support and pressing functions so each assembly operation can use an interface appropriate for the component being installed.

01 Support The shaft is held vertically with dedicated lower tooling beneath the hydraulic press.
02 Align Component-specific tooling establishes a controlled interface with the seal, carrier, or bearing being installed.
03 Press Axial force is applied through the dedicated tooling rather than through improvised contact surfaces.

Engineering Considerations

  • Shaft alignment beneath the hydraulic press
  • Axial load transfer through the tooling
  • Protection of seals and bearing surfaces
  • Tool accessibility during assembly
  • Compatibility with multiple assembly stages

Shop-Floor Integration

  • Dedicated tooling stored at the point of use
  • Labeled locations improve visual organization
  • Repeatable setup for recurring assemblies
  • Reduced reliance on improvised pressing methods
  • Clear separation between different tooling functions
