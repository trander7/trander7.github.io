Project 03 | Fixture Design & Assembly Control

Damper Shaft Assembly Press Tooling

Dedicated tooling developed to improve alignment, support, and repeatability during recurring damper shaft assembly operations.

Project Overview

Developed dedicated tooling for installing seal assemblies, bronze bearing carriers, and bearings onto damper drive and idle shafts.

The tooling supports the shaft beneath the hydraulic press and provides component-specific interfaces for controlled axial loading during the different stages of assembly.

Dedicated storage was also incorporated so the tooling required for recurring assembly operations could remain organized and available at the point of use.

Impact

Improved alignment, repeatability, setup organization, and control during recurring shaft-assembly operations.

Problem Addressed

  • Components could become misaligned during installation
  • Shafts required stable vertical support beneath the press
  • Different assembly stages required different tooling interfaces
  • Sensitive seal and bearing surfaces required controlled contact
  • Multiple pieces of tooling needed to remain organized and readily available

Design Response

  • Dedicated lower support for the damper shaft
  • Component-specific press interfaces
  • Tooling developed for multiple assembly stages
  • Controlled axial loading through the hydraulic press
  • Dedicated storage and organization for assembly tooling

Press Tooling & Organization

The tooling setup combines dedicated shaft support and press interfaces with organized storage for the tools used during recurring damper shaft assembly.

Assembly Press Setup

Hydraulic press setup with dedicated tooling used to support the shaft and control component installation.

Dedicated Tooling Storage

Dedicated storage keeps the component-specific press tooling organized, visible, and accessible at the point of use.

Assembly Approach

Step 01 Support

Position and support the damper shaft beneath the hydraulic press using the dedicated lower tooling.

Step 02 Align

Use the appropriate component-specific interface to maintain alignment during the applicable assembly operation.

Step 03 Press

Apply controlled axial loading through the hydraulic press to install the applicable shaft component.

Assembly Control

The dedicated tooling provides defined support and contact interfaces instead of relying on improvised tooling for recurring assembly operations. This helps maintain alignment while providing a controlled load path during pressing.

Point-of-Use Organization

Dedicated storage provides a defined location for the individual tooling components used during the assembly process. The arrangement improves visibility and access while helping keep the tooling together between uses.

Result

The completed setup provides a repeatable method for supporting damper shafts and installing seals, bearing carriers, and bearings using dedicated tooling rather than ad hoc assembly methods.

Portfolio Significance
