Project 05 | Equipment Protection

Shock-Absorbing Trolley End Stop

Integration of an industrial shock absorber into an overhead trolley-track end stop to reduce abrupt impact loading and protect trolley components.

Project Overview

Developed a shock-absorbing end-stop concept for overhead trolleys traveling on an I-beam track. If a manually positioned track switch is not changed, a loaded trolley can contact a rigid end stop and create an abrupt impact.

The design introduces a controlled deceleration stroke while retaining a positive mechanical stop behind the shock absorber.

Impact

Increased stopping distance, reduced peak impact loading, and lower risk of damage to trolley components.

Design Response

  • Industrial shock absorber aligned with trolley travel
  • Structural mount transferring reaction into the support frame
  • Dedicated contact surface for controlled engagement
  • Positive mechanical stop retained behind the absorber

Engineering Basis

The design uses additional stopping distance to dissipate trolley kinetic energy over a controlled stroke.

Selection and validation depend on moving mass, impact velocity, usable stroke, allowable force, impact frequency, and rated energy capacity.
